## Visitor Follow-Up & Returning Visitor Communication

We value your privacy and believe in transparent, permission-based communication. This section explains how we communicate with returning visitors to our website and web applications.

### 1. Permission-Based Contact Only
- **We only contact people who explicitly provided their email address to us.** For example, when you submit an integration request form, start a registration flow, or create an account.
- **We NEVER email anonymous visitors.** We do not use third-party identity resolution, data brokers, email-scraping scripts, or cold-contact databases.
- If you have never provided your email address directly to us, you will never receive an automated follow-up email from our service.

### 2. What Information We Use
When you return to our website after identifying yourself, our system may look at:
- The pages you explored on our website (such as our pricing section, documentation, or integration guides).
- General engagement signals (such as returning sessions or form interests).
- Business information you or your organization provided (such as your company name, domain, or selected CRM).
- Any notes or requirements you shared in previous forms.

This information is used strictly to understand your requirements and offer direct, helpful assistance (for example, sending technical documentation or answering questions about features you examined).

### 3. Strict Communication Frequency Limits
To respect your inbox:
- We enforce a **hard frequency cap** (typically at most one email every 7 days).
- We **never email existing paying customers** or team members with sales follow-ups.
- Once you reply to an email or tell us you are not interested, automated outreach ceases immediately.

### 4. CAN-SPAM Compliance & One-Click Unsubscribe
- Every email sent includes our legal physical mailing address in the footer.
- Every email includes a secure, one-click unsubscribe link (`List-Unsubscribe` header and footer link).
- Clicking the unsubscribe link permanently places your address on our suppression list. Once suppressed, our system enforces a hard code-level block preventing any future automated follow-up emails.

### 5. Third-Party Service Providers
When generating and sending communications, we may utilize trusted service providers acting under strict confidentiality and data protection agreements:
- **Cloudflare** (for secure, globally-distributed edge hosting and Workers AI inference)
- **Resend** (for transactional email delivery)
- **PostHog** (for product analytics, if opted in)

We never sell, rent, or lease your contact information to any third party.
